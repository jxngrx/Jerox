Three things, and then it gets out of the way.

Native

Built with Swift, SwiftUI, and AppKit. It requires macOS 15.1 or later. There is no Dock icon while you work.

Quiet

Open it with ⌃⌥⌘V, or from the menu bar. Escape or a click outside closes it and returns you to your app.

Yours

History stays on disk, on your Mac. Rephrase uses a key you keep in the Keychain. Nothing is sent until you ask.

How it works

  1. Open

    The list opens at the cursor, newest first. Press the shortcut again, press Escape, or click outside to dismiss it.

    ⌃⌥⌘V

  2. Search

    Type a phrase. An exact match ranks above a looser, character-by-character one.

  3. Paste

    Return pastes the row in its original form. Shift-Return pastes plain text. The keys 1 to 9 paste a row directly. Arrow keys move the selection.

    ↩ ⇧↩

  4. Pin

    Pin a row and it stays on top. Pins are exempt from your history limits, until you unpin them.

    ⌘P

Rephrase

It rewrites text so it sounds like a person wrote it. Three tones: Friendly chat, Professional chat, and Professional mail.

In the list, it rewrites the selected row. Anywhere else, select text and press ⌘⇧R. The result is pasted back in place.
