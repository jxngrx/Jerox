It works where you already are

Clipboard history, rewrite, and dictation for Mac. Each opens at your cursor and pastes back into the app you were using.

Copy in one app. Paste in another. The dot is your cursor. History, rewrite, dictate. One shortcut each. Pick a tone. The rewrite pastes back where you were. Speak. Text appears at your cursor. Every copy, kept on your Mac, newest first. Nothing leaves unless you ask for a rephrase. Jerox. It works where you already are.

Read the screen

Drag over anything you can see, an image, a video frame, a locked PDF, and its text appears right on it. Read on this Mac.

The Mac does the listening.

Dictation and screen reading run on this Mac. The network is used for one thing only: a rephrase you asked for.

On this Mac

History
Kept on your disk. Pins stay until you unpin them.
Dictation
Apple Speech on device, or a downloaded Whisper model.
Screen text
Read with on-device text recognition.
Rephrase
Apple Intelligence on macOS 26, no key needed.

Only when you ask

Rephrase
OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, or Hugging Face, with your own key.
Your keys
Stored in the macOS Keychain.
Speech models
Downloaded once, when you choose one.
